PE: Policy Editor

Define and maintain the controlled organisational policy variants used across Experiments E2–E6. Policy changes affect future experiment runs and must be versioned to preserve research reproducibility.

Research Design
The Policy Editor provides researchers with a controlled environment to define multiple policy variants and maintain version history. These policies are used as inputs in Experiments E2–E6 to evaluate model performance under different policy conditions.
Practical Example
Researchers create Clear, Vague, and Contradictory policy variants. Each variant is fully defined across all phases and rule categories. These variants are then used in experiments to assess model behaviour under policy uncertainty.
